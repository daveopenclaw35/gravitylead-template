How It Works

Our 5-Step Process

We've refined our installation process to deliver consistent, long-lasting results on every job.

1

Free Estimate

We inspect your floor, discuss options, and provide a detailed written quote — no pressure, no surprise fees.

2

Surface Prep

Diamond grinding, crack repair, and moisture testing. Proper prep is what separates a 2-year floor from a 20-year floor.

3

Primer Coat

Deep-penetrating primer that bonds to the concrete and seals against moisture vapor transmission.

4

Base & Broadcast

Color coat applied, decorative flake or metallic pigment broadcast to full saturation for complete coverage.

5

Topcoat & Cure

Polyaspartic or urethane topcoat applied for UV stability, abrasion resistance, and that showroom shine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most residential garage floors can be completed in 1–2 days depending on the system. Polyaspartic systems cure faster — often same-day. Epoxy systems typically require 24–48 hours before light foot traffic and 72 hours before vehicle traffic. We'll give you exact timing during your estimate.
With professional installation and proper prep, our coatings are built to last 15–25+ years. Longevity depends on the system chosen, traffic levels, and how the floor is maintained. We back our work with a written warranty. Specific warranty terms pending from Scott
Yes. Crack repair and joint filling are standard parts of our surface prep process. We grind, fill, and level the concrete before any coating goes down — because a great coating on a poorly prepped floor won't last.
Not when installed correctly. The #1 cause of epoxy failure is inadequate surface prep and cheap materials. We use industrial diamond grinders and commercial-grade coatings — not DIY kits. That's why our floors don't peel.
Standard epoxy can be slippery when wet, which is why we always recommend adding anti-slip additives to topcoats for garages, pool decks, and commercial applications. We'll discuss the right finish for your needs during consultation.
Epoxy is a two-part system that builds thickness and provides excellent chemical resistance — great as a base coat. Polyaspartic is a fast-cure UV-stable topcoat that's typically applied over epoxy. Polyaspartic-only systems cure in hours instead of days and offer superior UV resistance. We'll recommend the right system for your application and budget.
