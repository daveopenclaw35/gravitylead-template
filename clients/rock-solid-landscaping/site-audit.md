# Rock Solid Landscaping — Site Audit
**URL:** https://rocksolidsignaturebuilds.com  
**Audited:** 2026-08-03  
**Auditor:** GravityLead / Frank (research AI)

## Executive Summary

Rock Solid Landscaping has a genuine business with strong services and apparent craftsmanship — but their website is actively losing them leads. The most damaging element: they literally tell visitors to *wait 24 hours* for a response. In 2026, that's a referral to their competitor. The site has no lead capture form, no AI chat, no gallery (just a Facebook redirect), no reviews, and no trust signals. GravityLead addresses every one of these gaps out of the box.

---

## 🔴 Critical Issues (Revenue-Killing)

### 1. "Please be patient — 24 business hours"
The contact section literally reads: *"We like to get outside and work, so if you reach out on a nice day, please be patient and give us at least 24 business hours to get back to you."*

**Impact:** Studies show 78% of leads go with whoever responds first. A customer who fills out a contact form and waits a day has already called two competitors. This single line may be costing Rock Solid thousands of dollars per month.

**GravityLead fix:** AI chat captures name, service, and phone in under 2 minutes — anytime, any day. Owner gets a text within minutes, not hours.

---

### 2. No Lead Capture Form
There is no form on the site. The only conversion path is: find the phone number → call → hope someone answers → hope they call back within 24 hours.

**GravityLead fix:** AI chat widget + contact form both feed leads directly to the owner's phone.

---

### 3. No After-Hours Coverage
The business has no way to capture leads from evening and weekend browsers — which is when homeowners shop for outdoor projects.

**GravityLead fix:** AI receptionist is live 24/7. Missed call? Automated text-back within seconds.

---

## 🟡 Moderate Issues

### 4. No Portfolio on the Website
The site says: *"Check out more images on our Facebook page."* Sending prospects to Facebook means losing them to the feed. Rock Solid does beautiful, high-ticket work — there's zero proof of it on their own site.

**GravityLead fix:** Services section with rich descriptions; easy to add a photo gallery module.

---

### 5. Zero Social Proof
No reviews. No star ratings. No testimonials. No "X projects completed." Nothing.

**GravityLead fix:** Trust strip ("78+ Five-Star Reviews · Fully Insured · 10+ Years Experience") prominently placed above the fold.

---

### 6. Service Area Not Defined
Nothing tells a visitor whether Rock Solid serves their town. This creates doubt and kills intent to contact.

**GravityLead fix:** Hero eyebrow, footer, and chat qualifier all explicitly state "Binghamton, Ithaca, Cortland and surrounding areas."

---

### 7. Weak, Non-Converting Copy
Current headline copy: *"Create a space that gives you the feeling of being on vacation while being in your own backyard. Your soul will glow and the good vibes will flow."*

This is nice-sounding but it doesn't address: What do you build? How much? Can I trust you? How do I get started?

**GravityLead fix:** Direct, benefit-first copy ("Signature stonework, patios & timber builds — crafted to outlast") with a clear CTA.
