# GravityLead — Demo Call Script
**For:** Dave's brother (sales) · **Brand:** GravityLead · **getgravitylead.com**

> Goal of the call: get them to **watch the AI catch a lead live**, then offer a **free custom demo site**. Don't sell the website — sell the 24/7 lead-capture. Keep it conversational, not scripted-robot.

---

## 0. Before the call (30 seconds prep)
- Have the **demo site open** on your screen (`index.html`, set to their trade — HVAC/roofing/plumbing).
- Know their business name and one thing about them (from their Google/FB listing).
- To switch the demo to their trade: open `config.js`, change `ACTIVE_TRADE` to `"hvac"`, `"roofing"`, or `"plumbing"`, save, reload. Done.

---

## 1. Open (build rapport, 20 sec)
> "Hey [Name], this is [Your Name] — I help [HVAC/roofing/plumbing] businesses around [area] stop losing customers to missed calls. Got 90 seconds? I want to show you something, not pitch you."

*(If yes, go. If busy: "When's a better 5 minutes today or tomorrow?")*

---

## 2. The problem (agitate, 30 sec)
> "Quick question — when a customer calls you and you're on a job, up a ladder, or it's after hours… what happens to that call?"

*(Let them answer. Most say voicemail / they call back / it goes unanswered.)*

> "Right. And here's the kicker — most people who hit voicemail don't leave one. They just call the next guy. Studies say about 4 out of 10 calls to businesses like yours go unanswered. That's real jobs walking out the door."

---

## 3. The reveal — DEMO THE CHAT (the hook, 60 sec)
> "So here's what I built. This is a website with an AI assistant baked in. Watch this — I'm a customer landing on your site at 10pm."

**Live actions (share screen or describe):**
1. Click the **chat bubble** (bottom-right).
2. Bot greets: *"Hi! Is this an AC issue, heating issue, or a new install estimate?"*
3. Click a quick reply (e.g. **"AC not cooling"**).
4. Answer its questions — ZIP, urgency, phone number.
5. It confirms: *"I've logged your request and a tech will text you within 15 minutes."*

> "See what just happened? At 10pm, with nobody in the office, it greeted the customer, figured out what they needed, qualified them, grabbed their number — and it would've texted YOU that lead instantly. That customer's booked instead of calling your competitor."

---

## 4. Why it matters to THEM (translate to money, 30 sec)
> "What's one job worth to you — a few hundred? A few thousand? This thing runs 24/7 and doesn't miss. If it catches even one job a month you'd have lost, it's already paid for itself many times over."

---

## 5. The offer (free demo close, 30 sec)
> "Here's what I'd like to do — no cost, no commitment. Let me build you a free preview of this for [Business Name]. Your name, your services, the AI already trained on [their trade]. I'll send you a link in a couple days. If you love it, we make it live — most folks go with our Done-For-You package, $1,997 to set up and $149 a month for hosting, the AI, and support. If it's not for you, no worries, you keep nothing owed. Fair?"

*(Get a yes to the FREE DEMO — that's the win. The close happens when they see their own site.)*

---

## 6. Capture their info (do this before hanging up)
- Business name (exact)
- Their cell (to text the demo link)
- Email
- Trade + top 2-3 services they want featured
- Service area / city

> "Perfect. I'll have your demo ready by [day]. Talk soon, [Name]."

---

## Tone Reminders
- You're **showing**, not selling. The demo does the selling.
- Talk about **their lost jobs**, not "features."
- Short sentences. Let them react. Ask questions.
- If they're not interested: "No problem — mind if I send the free demo anyway? Costs you nothing to look." (Often flips a no.)
